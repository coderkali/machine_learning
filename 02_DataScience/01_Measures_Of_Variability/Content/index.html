Measures Of Variability

Two datasets can share an average and describe completely different worlds.

2 notebooks✍️ 2 handwritten pages

The problem behind this topic

Two teams both averaged 50 marks. On paper they looked identical.

Team A scored 48, 50, 51, 51. Team B scored 5, 20, 80, 95. Same average, utterly different classrooms.

The average hid the only thing that mattered — the spread.

Variance measures how far the values sit from the mean, on average. Standard deviation is its square root, which puts the answer back into marks instead of marks-squared.

🔑 The one idea

The mean tells you where the middle is. Variance and standard deviation tell you how far people are from it.
