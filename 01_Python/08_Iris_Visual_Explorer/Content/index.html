See the connections

Correlation shows which measurements move together

Values near +1 move together, values near −1 move in opposite directions, and values near 0 have a weak linear relationship. Hover a cell for an explanation; click it to update the scatter plot.

Important data lesson

The group you choose can change the relationship.

Petal length and petal width have a very strong correlation of about 0.96 across all flowers. Filter to one species and the value changes because you are comparing flowers within one cluster instead of across three clusters.

Read the pattern

Three things to remember

01

Petals separate species best

Setosa petal length stops at 1.9 cm, while Versicolor starts at 3.0 cm.

02

Some species overlap

Versicolor and Virginica share a boundary region, so one measurement is not always enough.

03

Sepal width tells a different story

Its overall relationships are weak or negative, unlike the strong petal connection.
