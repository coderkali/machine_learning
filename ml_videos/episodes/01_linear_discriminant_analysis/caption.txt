One line can tell them apart. 🩺

A biopsy gives you 30 measurements — and you need one word: malignant or benign. Look at any single measurement alone and the two groups blur together. Linear Discriminant Analysis finds the ONE direction where the two groups pull apart the most, then projects every point onto it. Verified by hand on a 4-point example, and matched against scikit-learn on 569 real biopsy samples.
