Decisions on the record

Every real choice becomes a file in docs/decisions/, so that in three months the reason is still on disk. All five are accepted. Two of them are parked until the project widens beyond one station.

accepted D-001 · DAF-02

Predict the number, then apply the AQI table

Chosen: PM2.5 in µg/m³, with the CPCB table read off it afterwards.

Why: a category-only model makes 91 and 400 the same answer. The number keeps the severity and still gives the yes or no.

accepted D-002 · DAF-02

Tomorrow's 24-hour mean, 00:00–23:59 IST

Chosen: the full day, not the 07:00–10:00 assembly window.

Why: the CPCB bands are defined on the full day, so the number means the same thing to everyone. A morning target can come later.

accepted parked D-003 · DAF-03 · 16 Sep

Keep stations with 730+ days of real coverage

Chosen: coverage_days = last − first, keep if ≥ 730. 50 stations pass.

Why: objective, easy to explain, and based on elapsed time rather than calendar years.

The record says it plainly: a minimum quality gate, not the final word. It also says the project should then prefer the newest 3–4 years — which is what D-004 picks up.

accepted parked D-004 · DAF-04 · 16 Sep

Train on the most recent 3–4 years

Chosen: the latest four years inside the valid pool — 2022-09-16 → 2026-09-16. 44 of the 50 survive.

Why: Delhi's traffic, fuel and policy have changed. A model for tomorrow should learn the current regime, not 2016.

Filed under ticket DAF-04, though DAF-04 on the board is the S3 collector. Worth a look when the ticket opens.

accepted D-005 · DAF-04 · 18 Sep

Build version one on a single station — R K Puram

The problem it answers: D-003 rewards a long history and D-004 a recent one. OpenAQ re-issued the CPCB feed in late 2022, so together they kept a retired sensor over its live replacement at 37 of the 44 stations. The evidence →

Chosen: one station, all the way to a running service — R K Puram (location 17), already downloaded, verified, and on the live sensor. 488 valid days, 156 of them Poor or worse.

Why: the project is for learning the whole path. One station gets a real model error in three tickets, and every later data decision is judged by that error. D-003 and D-004 are parked, not wrong; DAF-24 revisits them.
